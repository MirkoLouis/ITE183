Realms of Etheryn
⚔   An Age of Shadow Awaits   ⚔

In the age before memory, the realm of Etheryn flourished under the light of the Celestial Accord — a pact forged between mortals, spirits, and the ancient dragons. Peace reigned for a thousand years.

But the shadow came without warning.

Sephiron, once a revered paladin of the Order of Dawn, was corrupted by the Voidstone buried beneath the Doomwood Forest. Its whispers reached the goblin tribes of the wood first. Now a bloated Goblin King rules them from the Deeper Forest, and his raiders creep closer to the last free town every night.

The Celestial Accord is broken. The dragons have withdrawn. The Order of Dawn is ash.

You are what remains.

A hero answers the call not because they are unafraid, but because someone must. Your legend begins in Etheryn Town, where Captain Seraphel gathers the willing, and the merchants of the Market arm them for the dark woods beyond.

Will you break the goblin horde, or will Sephiron's shadow swallow the last light?
